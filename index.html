Industrial PMM, Storytelling & Enablement

Cecillia Lescano

I gravitate toward complicated products. The technical kind, usually, with long sales cycles, lots of acronyms, and a tendency to be harder to explain than they should be. I’ve spent a good part of my career figuring out what’s actually interesting about them, how to tell that story, and how to get it in front of the people who need to hear it. As it turns out, even good products need someone to explain why anyone should care.
