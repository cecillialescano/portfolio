CarboNet for Oil & Gas — pitch at Oilfield Water Markets

A conference-stage narrative that reframed industrial water-treatment chemistry as a credibility play for skeptical oil & gas technical buyers.

Client CarboNet
Format Conference-stage pitch
Audience Oil & gas technical buyers
The Situation

Water-treatment chemistry is a hard sell from a stage

CarboNet needed a pitch for Oilfield Water Markets — a room of technical buyers who evaluate water-treatment claims for a living and have little patience for vendor overstatement. The underlying chemistry was genuinely differentiated, but chemistry alone doesn't make a stage narrative.

The challenge was translating a technical advantage into a story a skeptical, expert audience would actually trust, in the few minutes a conference stage allows.

The Approach

Framing the chemistry as a credibility play, not a features list

Rather than leading with specifications, the narrative was built around what a technical buyer actually needs to believe before they'll take a claim seriously — and structured the pitch to earn that belief in sequence.

  • Opened with the buyer's own skepticism rather than the product, naming the overpromising the audience had already learned to distrust.
  • Sequenced technical proof points to build credibility progressively, rather than front-loading every claim at once.
  • Translated dense treatment chemistry into language a non-chemist evaluator on a buying committee could still follow and repeat internally.
