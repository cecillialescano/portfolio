Voltstack field stories: proof from the jobsite

Customer-story content built to make an unfamiliar category tangible — real crews, real jobsites, told in their own words.

Client Portable Electric
Format Website home page
Audience Jobsite crews & equipment buyers
The Situation

A new category, and no shorthand for what it does

Voltstack, Portable Electric's jobsite power platform, was introducing a category buyers didn't have an easy mental shortcut for yet. Specs and product shots could show what the equipment was — they couldn't show what it changed on an actual jobsite.

The home page needed to do more than describe the product. It needed to make an unfamiliar category feel proven, fast, to a buyer who trusts what other crews say more than what a spec sheet claims.

The Approach

Letting the crews carry the story

The approach was to build the home page's narrative around real field stories rather than product copy — using the people who'd actually used the equipment on real jobsites as the proof, in their own words.

  • Structured the home page around field stories instead of a feature list, so the first thing a visitor meets is proof, not a pitch.
  • Kept the language grounded in how crews actually talk about equipment on a jobsite, not marketing shorthand.
  • Used specific jobsites and use cases rather than generic testimonials, so the proof felt verifiable rather than staged.
