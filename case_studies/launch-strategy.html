Manifest Agent: go-to-market for an AI product line

End-to-end launch plan for an agentic product entering a skeptical enterprise buyer set.

Category Product Launch
Role Lead PMM
Focus Positioning, launch sequencing, sales readiness
01 — The Situation

An agentic product, and a buyer set that doesn't trust agents yet

Manifest Agent was entering a market where "AI agent" already meant something to buyers — mostly overpromised demos and unclear accountability. The product itself was ready. The go-to-market motion, positioning, and internal sales narrative were not.

The job was to build a launch that earned credibility with a technical, skeptical enterprise buyer, rather than one that leaned on category hype.

02 — The Approach

Sequencing the launch around proof, not announcement

Rather than a single launch moment, the plan was built in phases — establishing internal alignment first, then building external proof points, then opening the announcement to a wider audience once the story could hold up under scrutiny.

  • Aligned product, sales, and leadership on a single narrative and set of proof points before any external messaging went out.
  • Built the core positioning around what the product could reliably do today, not a roadmap promise.
  • Sequenced a phased rollout — private beta narrative, then case-study-backed announcement, then broad launch — so each stage had evidence behind it.
  • Equipped sales with objection-handling material addressing the specific trust gap around agentic products.
